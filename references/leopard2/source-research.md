# Reference identification and supplemental geometry

Research date: 2026-09-09. User-supplied drawing remains the primary geometry source.

## Identification

The supplied caption reads `Figure 2, Leopard 2 tank, four-view drawing` (Chinese). The most defensible interpretation is an early Leopard 2 prototype with the original welded, spaced-armour turret, circa 1971-1975. An exact PT chassis / T turret number cannot be established from this scan.

Do not label it Leopard 1A3/1A4 solely on turret resemblance. The large 3.700 m width, 7.672 m hull envelope, seven-wheel chassis, twin circular rear deck fans and caption support Leopard 2. The original prototype turret deliberately resembles the Leopard 1A4 turret. Do not replace it with the substantially different series-production Leopard 2A4 turret or the later wedge-front 2A5 turret.

## Modeling observations and sensible interpolation

- Sloped lower bow, broad nearly horizontal engine deck, projecting track fenders and a long chamfered rear hull.
- Low angular welded turret with clipped front corners and a long rear bustle. Its shape is visible in all four views and should be traced before adding small details.
- Seven paired, dished rubber-rimmed road wheels per side, smaller elevated front idlers and toothed rear drive sprockets.
- Rear drive sprocket, front idler and largely straight lower track run; upper track obscured in places by fenders and stowage panels.
- Stepped main tube with a prominent fume extractor and no muzzle brake. The scan alone does not establish whether this prototype carries the 105 mm or 120 mm gun; prototype sources document both.
- Two circular rear-deck fan grilles side by side, longitudinal radiator/engine grilles and angular housings. Circular fan grilles are explicit in top view.
- Turret-mounted machine gun, two roof hatches, several optical/periscope boxes and a tall slender rear equipment mast.
- Each side of the turret shows two rows of four angled smoke-grenade launchers. Model the visible exterior tubes only.
- External tow ropes, handles, lifting eyes, tool clamps and stowage boxes are visible but have no numerical dimensions. Their thicknesses, bevels and attachment geometry are visual estimates.
- Track pitch, link count, bolt count, panel thickness, exact wheel inner dish section and hidden underside geometry are not dimensioned. Use plausible symmetric exterior geometry and identify it as estimated, rather than claiming blueprint/manufacturing precision.
